# Examples (illustrative only)

## Inflated language
Before: The implementation of digital archiving practices has facilitated the establishment of new possibilities for the preservation of cultural heritage.
After: Digital archives create new ways to preserve cultural heritage.

## Rhetorical reversal
Before: It is not simply about preserving objects, but about protecting cultural identity.
After: Protecting cultural identity is an important part of heritage preservation.

## Mechanical enumerations
Before: Firstly, archives preserve records. Secondly, they support participation. Furthermore, they increase access.
After: Archives help preserve records. They can also support community participation and make materials easier to access.

These are style illustrations, not universally correct replacements: keep the nuance needed by actual evidence and context.
